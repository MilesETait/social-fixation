Social Media Marketing

Let's talk

We build social around what the business actually needs it to do. Brand building, demand, content, community and distribution work differently, so the strategy, creative and channel activity need to work as one system rather than a collection of posts.

01 · What it is

What is social media marketing?

Social is bigger than the content calendar.

Social media marketing is the coordinated use of social channels to help a brand achieve defined marketing and business objectives.

It can bring together strategy, channel management, content, community, creator partnerships and paid distribution, with each discipline playing a different role depending on what the brand is trying to change.

01 / 03

The mistake is treating all of that as one job called “posting on social”.

Social can help a brand become easier to recognise. It can keep a product present between campaigns. It can reach people who do not know the brand yet. It can give existing customers another reason to care. It can distribute a campaign, create useful signals for paid media or give the marketing team an ongoing way to respond to what is happening around the business.

Those are different jobs, and they do not all require the same content, platform, cadence or investment. A brand building long-term recognition should make different decisions from one launching a new product next month.

02 / 03

That also means knowing what not to do.

  • Not every brand needs every platform.
  • Not every campaign needs an influencer.
  • Not every piece of content needs paid support.
  • More posts are not automatically the answer to weak performance.

We work out what social needs to contribute, then build the right combination of strategy, management, creative, creators and distribution around it.

03 / 03

02 · What it can do

Different jobs need different social.

01

Build recognition

Social can make a brand easier to notice, recognise and remember over time.

That requires more than keeping an account active. The brand needs repeatable ideas, distinctive creative signals and enough consistency for individual pieces of content to reinforce something larger. The job is cumulative: each interaction gives people another chance to understand who the brand is and why it matters.

03

Build participation

Some of social's value comes from giving people a way to respond rather than simply receive a message.

That can mean conversation, community activity, creator involvement, customer participation or content people actively want to share and contribute to. The appropriate level depends on the brand, but participation can make the audience part of how an idea travels instead of leaving every piece of reach to the brand itself.

02

Create demand

Social can give people new reasons to consider a brand, product or proposition.

A launch might need explanation. An established product might need a fresh reason to enter consideration. A brand may need to demonstrate usefulness, make an unfamiliar benefit easier to understand or stay visible until the buying moment arrives. The creative and distribution should reflect the specific change required rather than treating every objective as an awareness problem.

04

Turn attention into action

Attention only matters commercially when it can contribute to the next useful step.

Depending on the business, that might mean a site visit, product trial, enquiry, store visit, purchase or another defined action. Paid distribution can put more weight behind the right activity, while stronger creative gives people a clearer reason to act. Not every social programme needs to drive an immediate conversion. But when action is the job, the system should be designed accordingly.

06 · How we work

Four habits we don't break.

  1. 01

    Start with the business problem

    We do not begin by deciding how many posts, videos or platforms a brand should have.

    We start with what needs to change and where social can make a useful contribution.

  2. 02

    Build the right operating model

    Some clients need a strategic project. Some need ongoing management. Some already have internal teams and need additional creative or specialist support.

    The engagement should reflect the actual gap rather than forcing every client into the same package.

  3. 03

    Keep the disciplines connected

    Strategy is stronger when the people responsible for execution understand it. Creative improves when production understands how and where the work will be used. Management improves when content and performance are not treated as separate conversations.

    We keep those connections close enough that the work does not lose its logic between handovers.

  4. 04

    Learn and adjust

    Social changes quickly, but that does not mean chasing every trend or rebuilding the strategy every month.

    We use what the channels, audience and work are telling us to make better subsequent decisions while keeping the larger brand and commercial job in view.

07 · FAQs

Questions about social media marketing

What does a social media marketing agency do?+

A social media marketing agency helps brands plan and execute marketing activity across social platforms. Depending on the requirement, that can involve strategy, channel management, content creation, publishing, community management, creator partnerships, paid social advertising and performance review. The right scope depends on what the business actually needs social to accomplish.

What does social media marketing include?+

Social media marketing can include audience and channel strategy, organic channel management, content production, community activity, creator and influencer campaigns, paid social advertising, publishing and performance analysis. It does not mean every brand needs every discipline. A useful social programme combines the parts required for the specific marketing objective.

Do you manage social media as well as create content?+

Yes. Social Media Management and Social Media Content Creation are separate services that can work together or independently. Management operates and coordinates the channels; Content Creation makes the creative assets those channels need. Brands with internal production can use management without outsourcing content, while other teams may need both.

Do you handle paid social advertising?+

Yes, paid social can form part of the wider social marketing mix where it is appropriate to the objective. Paid campaign strategy, targeting, budgets, media buying and optimisation are distinct from organic channel management, so we treat paid execution as its own discipline rather than hiding it inside a general management scope.

Which social media platforms do you work across?+

The appropriate platform mix depends on the brand, audience and objective. That can include Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn and other relevant social environments. We do not recommend maintaining every available channel simply because it exists; each platform should have a clear reason to be part of the mix.

How much does social media marketing cost?+

The cost depends on the scope. A defined strategy project, monthly publishing support, ongoing channel management, regular content production and a paid-media programme require very different levels of work. We scope the engagement around the disciplines, production requirements and ongoing responsibility the brand actually needs rather than applying one universal social media package.

Do we need a social media strategy before management starts?+

Not always. If the brand already has a clear, current strategy, management can operate against it. If the audience, role of social, channel priorities or content direction remain unresolved, establishing the strategy first usually gives the ongoing activity a stronger basis for decision-making.

Can you work with our internal marketing team?+

Yes. Social media marketing does not need to be entirely outsourced. We can take responsibility for a defined part of the system, work alongside an internal marketing team or coordinate with other specialist partners. The important thing is making responsibility clear so work does not disappear between teams.
